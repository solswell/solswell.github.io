## 01-open-swell

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: premium SOLSWELL brand direction concept board for approval, landscape aspect ratio.
Primary request: Design an original master wave symbol whose FIRST read is a strong side-view ocean swell, SECOND read is rising market momentum. The swell must rise from a low, flowing waterline into an elevated crest, with an unmistakable curling ocean-wave silhouette and generous negative space. Momentum is implied by the accelerating contour and current flow, never by literal financial graphics.
Brand: SOLSWELL represents the whole Solana ocean. Sea creatures are secondary supporting archetypes, never the logo. Premium, sharp, energetic, sophisticated.
Board layout: polished dark navy editorial board. Left third: a large simple flat/vector-style master wave mark; a small white one-color version beneath proving the silhouette works; a row of four small palette swatches. Right two-thirds: a cinematic hero-art application of the same wave geometry, with a clean SOLSWELL wordmark and slogan in the negative space. Restrained margins and crisp typesetting. The board is a concept presentation, not a website redesign.
Text verbatim: "SOLSWELL" and "Ride the chop. Catch the swell." Only these words plus the direction title specified below. Spell SOLSWELL S-O-L-S-W-E-L-L.
Color family: deep ocean navy and blue base; green, cyan and purple accents. Bright colors trace water currents rather than filling the whole board. Maintain strong dark-light contrast.
Constraints: no candlesticks, chart axes, bars, ticker tapes, literal upward arrows, coins, currency signs, financial app badges, official Solana logo, Solana wordmark, endorsement seals, or watermarks. No shark-shaped logo, no mascot face in the mark, no generic swoosh, no childish styling. The wave must read cleanly as a single ocean symbol at small avatar sizes. Keep the logo and hero-wave silhouette consistent.
Direction title: "01 / OPEN SWELL"
Concept: pure wave first, timeless and elemental. A broad continuous ocean swell with a substantial blue body, a low long tapering waterline, and one clean curled crest. A gently increasing slope followed by a tighter rising curve suggests accumulated momentum. There is no pointed arrow tip.
Logo: minimal single dominant wave mass with one carved negative-space curl and a restrained cyan inner current. Elegant asymmetric balance, oceanic rather than sporty or finance-generic.
Wordmark: custom upright broad uppercase geometric sans, confident medium-wide proportions, carefully spaced, subtle smooth wave-derived curve in the W, no italic racing style.
Color emphasis: midnight blue #061725, swell blue #0B4C7A, cyan #39E6F3 dominant accent, small green #28D9A1 and violet #8B5CF6 traces.
Hero art: one immense sculptural blue swell in side profile on dark water, calm luminous cyan edge and a few flowing green/purple data-like currents inside the water. Restrained cinematic realism, lots of navy negative space. No creatures in this direction.
