# SOLSWELL master wave — three directions for review

Slogan: **Ride the chop. Catch the swell.**

The wave is the master identity. Sol the shark, whales, turtles, squids and other archetypes belong in the supporting ocean world. All directions imply momentum through the shape and flow of water rather than financial graphics. No official Solana logo or affiliation cues are used.

## 1. Open Swell — ocean first

**Concept:** Quiet confidence gathering into a powerful swell. The smooth rising shoulder suggests momentum building before the crest; the first read remains an ocean wave.

**Logo/icon:** One broad side-view swell, a generous negative-space curl and a long low waterline. The blue body carries the identity; cyan defines the crest. The production icon would remove fine interior cuts at small sizes and retain the single strongest curl.

**Wordmark:** Upright, broad uppercase lettering with balanced spacing. A subtle curve in the W can echo the swell without becoming a picture or arrow.

**Color emphasis:** Midnight navy and ocean blue; cyan leads, green and purple appear as restrained currents.

**Hero art:** One monumental sculptural swell with luminous currents running through its body. Deep navy space keeps the name and slogan readable. The water itself is the protagonist.

**Best fit:** The clearest foundation for a lasting master symbol. This is my recommended base identity.

## 2. Breakwater — wave with breakout force

**Concept:** Pressure builds through rough water and releases into a surging crest. A steeper rising shoulder, tighter curl and accelerating currents make market momentum more noticeable while retaining an ocean silhouette.

**Logo/icon:** A taller, sharper asymmetric wave with one short organic spray cut. The crest carries green/cyan energy; purple trails along the base. The small-size version would omit detached spray and fine ribbons.

**Wordmark:** Moderately condensed uppercase lettering with a slight forward lean and crisp terminal cuts. The W stays within the normal cap height; it does not become an arrow.

**Color emphasis:** Deep blue base, stronger electric green and cyan at the crest, purple in the wake.

**Hero art:** A powerful swell rising out of lower choppy waves, with current streams accelerating up its face. Stronger spray, motion and contrast than Open Swell.

**Best fit:** The most energetic launch and campaign treatment; the least restrained of the three.

## 3. One Ocean — wave as the shared world

**Concept:** Many currents contribute to one swell. The wave stands for the whole ecosystem; the creatures express different ways of moving through it.

**Logo/icon:** A compact side-view curl with a broad open center and two subordinate current ribbons. No animal shapes inside the mark. The icon would simplify to the wave and one supporting current at tiny sizes.

**Wordmark:** Upright, wider uppercase letters with open counters and generous spacing. Confident and inclusive rather than aggressive.

**Color emphasis:** Deep navy and blue, with cyan, green and purple currents sharing the accent role.

**Hero art:** A dramatic swell above a layered underwater world. Shark, whale, turtle and squid remain smaller supporting figures, connected by luminous currents. The wave remains the largest visual element.

**Best fit:** Community storytelling, gallery scenes and ecosystem campaigns.

## Recommendation

Choose Open Swell for the master mark, with One Ocean as its supporting art language. That gives SOLSWELL a recognizable ocean symbol while preserving the wider community. Breakwater is available if the preferred tone is more forceful and focused on momentum.
