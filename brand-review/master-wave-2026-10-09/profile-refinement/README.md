- Reduced the scale-like treatment around the turning face while retaining crisp cinematic water detail.
- Kept foam and spray concentrated at the breaking right lip and lower-right impact area.
- Preserved the natural wave silhouette, cool mist background, green/cyan/purple lighting, and the complete uneven rising candlestick sequence.
- Kept the candlesticks legible but secondary to the ocean.

- **128px:** Wave silhouette, barrel, crest and full rising candle sequence are clearly readable.
