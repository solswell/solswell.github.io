# V18 — natural water with isolated arcs

V18 uses the existing V14 natural-water banner as its untouched base. It does not use image generation or a water-wide lighting treatment.

Only three independently composited smooth arcs are added inside the primary swell: cyan follows the inner barrel, green stays closely paired to it, and restrained purple follows the lower barrel curve.

No colour grading, water replacement, point field, texture overlay, or distributed glow is applied. The source remains unchanged at `../master-banner-v14-natural-water/solswell-v14-natural-water-master.png`.

`build_v18.py` creates the master image, website crop, X crop, and source-versus-treated comparison.
