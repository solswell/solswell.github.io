## Master artwork

![SOLSWELL master banner](solswell-master-banner.png)

One wide cinematic scene extends the approved Cool Mist V4 identity into the broader SOLSWELL ocean. The dominant natural swell carries the luminous current language and rising green candle sequence. Distant swells and underwater silhouettes remain atmospheric so the main wave reads first.

The left side and upper-left retain readable negative space for later placement of **SOLSWELL** and **Ride the chop. Catch the swell.** No text is baked into the artwork.

## Visual checks

- Primary swell dominates the composition and preserves the V4 wave-first identity.
- Green/cyan/purple currents and the uneven rising candles imply momentum without a pasted chart overlay.
- Distant swells are faint and non-literal; SOL/RAY references do not imply affiliation or partnership.
- Whale, shark, turtle, squid and schooling fish are shadowy underwater atmosphere, not mascots or a collage.
- Deep blues and Cool Mist lighting are preserved; no warm sunset palette, official Solana marks, text or watermarks.
- V4 profile artwork remains untouched.
